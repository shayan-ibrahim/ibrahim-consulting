The Problem

Why good companies get stuck growing.

I've sat inside growing companies long enough to know — it's rarely a talent problem. It's a structure problem. You've grown past the informal way of doing things, but haven't built what comes next.

  • 01

    Nobody's sure who owns what

    Roles blur, decisions stall, and accountability falls through the cracks every time.

  • 02

    Your leaders are stuck firefighting

    When every day is reactive, strategy never gets off the ground — ever.

  • 03

    Teams pulling in different directions

    Everyone's working hard, just not toward the same goal or outcome.

  • 04

    Money walking out the door

    Duplicated effort, broken handoffs, and unclear processes quietly drain your bottom line.

  • 05

    No two locations run the same

    Results depend on who's managing that day — and that's a problem you can't scale out of.

  • 06

    You've outgrown how you operate

    The company grew — fast. But the foundation never caught up, and now everything feels harder than it should.

Approach

How we actually get this done.

No guessing games. Every engagement moves through the same three phases — and at each step, you'll know exactly what's happening and why.

Phase 01

We listen first.

Before recommending anything, we get inside your business. We talk to your people, observe how things actually run, and find what's really getting in the way.

  • Executive and leadership interviews
  • Department and workflow observations
  • Organizational structure gap review
  • Waste and inefficiency analysis
  • Strategic findings and gap report
Phase 02

We build the fix.

No templates dropped on your desk. We design solutions around how your business actually works — org structures, frameworks, and roadmaps you can actually use.

  • Organizational redesign and structure
  • Job descriptions and department overviews
  • Process flows and accountability frameworks
  • Future-state operating model
  • Prioritized implementation roadmap
Phase 03

We stay until it works.

We don't hand over a deck and disappear. We stay in it — coaching, adjusting, following up — until the changes are real and your team owns them.

  • Leadership and manager coaching
  • Facilitated team alignment sessions
  • Implementation planning and support
  • Performance review delivery
  • Accountability and follow-up sessions

Real experience. Real results.

My name is Shayan Ibrahim [pronounced shy-awn]. If you're looking for someone who genuinely understands what it feels like to be inside a growing company trying to figure things out, you've found the right person. I've led at the highest levels of corporate America and brought that knowledge to growing businesses. I've spent 18 years not just advising businesses, but actually running them. This work isn't abstract to me. It's personal and where I excel.

I grew up as a Pakistani American in South Louisiana, where my parents instilled in me a sense of hard work and doing right by others — it's how I operate. It's also where I met my best friend, who is now my fiancée. These values helped build a foundation within me on how I approach relationships: with commitment, care, and a long-term mindset.

I started my career with Apple, then joined AT&T where I spent over a decade building things that lasted. As Chief of Staff to C-Suite leaders, I helped lead departments responsible for billions in revenue and supported strategy across 5,700+ retail locations nationwide. I didn't just learn how great companies operate, I helped design it.

From there, I moved into the Authorized Retail space as Vice President, overseeing 1,100+ locations and 2,000+ employees during my career. I've successfully supported 17 Authorized Retailers, helping each one build structure, scale profitably, and operate with the discipline of an enterprise while keeping the soul of a small business.

I went to school for Architecture. I loved the systems logic and design thinking, but quickly realized my true passion was people. I've carried that same structural mindset into every phase of my career and business I've touched since. What I bring to Ibrahim Consulting is what I wish more of my clients had access to earlier — strategic clarity, operational rigor, and a people-first approach that makes growth feel less like chaos and more like momentum.
